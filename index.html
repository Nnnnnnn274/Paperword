    A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

    Before you drop it in.

    What can I edit?

    Add text annotations to PDFs, rotate or remove pages, reorder pages, and combine multiple PDFs. Open a Word document or start a new one, then edit text, headings, lists, and basic formatting. PDF annotations add a layer of text; existing PDF text is not replaced. Word import/export simplifies complex layouts and omits images.

    Will my document look exactly the same?

    Paperword preserves text, page breaks, and basic text sizing. Complex layouts, columns, tables, images, and special fonts may change or be omitted. Review your Word document after conversion.

    Can I convert scanned PDFs?

    This converter works with selectable text. Scanned pages are images and need optical character recognition (OCR), which isn’t included. We’ll flag pages without text so you know what to expect.

    Where do my files go?

    Nowhere. Conversion happens on your device, entirely within this browser tab. Your PDF and Word document aren’t uploaded or stored by Paperword.

    Which files can I use?

    Choose a text-based PDF up to 25 MB and 200 pages. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first. Your download is a .docx file compatible with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice.
